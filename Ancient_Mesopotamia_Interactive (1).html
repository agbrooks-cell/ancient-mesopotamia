Land Between Two Rivers

Mesopotamia means “land between the rivers.” The Tigris and Euphrates helped provide water for farming and transportation.

🏙️

Sumerian City-States

Sumer was made up of independent city-states such as Ur, Uruk, and Lagash. Each had its own government and ruler.

👑

Government & Society

Kings ruled city-states. Sumerian society included farmers, craftspeople, merchants, priests, scribes, and enslaved people.

🌾

Farming & Irrigation

Sumerians built canals and irrigation systems to move river water to fields. Farming produced food that supported growing cities.
